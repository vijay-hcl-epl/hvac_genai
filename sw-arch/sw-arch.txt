SOFTWARE ARCHITECTURE DOCUMENT – HVAC FLAP ACTUATOR (MOTIX™ TLE9851QXW)

1.0 ARCHITECTURE STYLE SELECTION

Candidate styles:
- Layered
- Component-based (modular, functional partitions)
- Monolithic

Evaluation:
- System complexity: Modest; no explicit concurrency or dynamic behaviors, single-function actuator.
- HW interaction: Requires clean separation for MCU, ADC, UART/LIN, GPIO, and motor driver.
- Testability: Needs independent unit testing (sectioned logic, stubs/mocks feasible for I/O).
- Maintainability: Future extensibility needed (implied by system and software reqs).
- ASPICE L3: Modular and interface-based mapping required; monolithic rejected.

Style Decision:
Selected: Component-based (modular, interface-centric) architecture.
Rejected: Layered (too heavy/simple for 1-actuator scope); Monolithic (no isolation/extendibility).

Trace: All decisions justified by system and software requirements documents (SWE-REQ-041, SWE-REQ-042, SWE-REQ-043).
