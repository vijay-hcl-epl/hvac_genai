## 1. Architecture Style Selection and Justification

### Candidates considered
- **Monolithic (main-loop only):** Simple, hard to maintain/trace, fails requirements for separation and explicit responsibility.
- **Layered (Application/HAL):** Too complex/formal for requirements, unjustified abstraction for a prototype/learning system.
- **Component-based (modular, direct):** Matches needs for clear boundaries, testability, traceability, ASPICE alignment, and maintainability for a microcontroller firmware prototype.

**Selected style:**
- **Component-based modular architecture**

**Justification:**
- Requirement-driven explicit separation (SYS-REQ-049, SWE-REQ-035, SWE-REQ-048, et al).
- Clear, maintainable assignment of responsibilities.
- Unit-to-requirement traceability.
- Avoids unnecessary complexity/layers.
- Facilitates testing (SWE.1, SYS-REQ-050/051).
